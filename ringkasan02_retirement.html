RETIREMENT IS NOT THE END OF THE JOURNEY


Kresnawan Sasmita Putra

Summary
  Retirement Is Not the End of the Journey is the English version of the author's reflections on life after retirement.   The book grew from personal notes and reflections collected through the Kutipan Pustaka blog since 2009. Those scattered writings were reorganized into a book about continuing to learn, work, create, and seek meaning after leaving formal employment.   Rather than treating retirement as the end of productivity, the book presents it as a new stage of life—a period in which a person can rediscover time, knowledge, faith, creativity, and personal purpose.   Its message is particularly relevant to people who feel uncertain after retirement. The loss of a job title or routine does not necessarily mean the loss of one's usefulness. New activities, digital literacy, writing, entrepreneurship, and sharing knowledge can become meaningful ways of continuing the journey.   The book ultimately encourages readers to keep learning, creating, contributing, and drawing closer to Allah.   Core message:   > Retirement closes one chapter of employment, but it does not have to close the book of life.
